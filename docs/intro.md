# Rebel Badge Club

Welcome to my Rebel Badge Club documentation! This is a Docusaurus site, which means it's built using the Docusaurus framework. Docusaurus is a powerful tool for creating documentation websites, and it provides a great way to organize and present information about your project.

I am using Docusaurus to create a fun and engaging documentation site for my Rebel Badge Club badges.

_All Rebel Badge Club assets and details are owned by Charly Lester._ 

## What is the Rebel Badge Club?
Rebel Badge Book is made of 52 different badges, and corresponding syllabuses that you have to complete, to get the badge.  
In November 2022 a second follow-on book was release - Rebel Badge Book Volume Two (it has a green  cover).  
In November 2024, Volume 3 (with a purple cover) was published.

The topics are many and varied (check out the lists below!) spanning Crafts, Hobbies, Sports, Wellness, the Outdoors, and lots more!

## Badges and how to achieve them
There are three different books, each with 52 badges. To earn a badge, you need to complete the syllabus for that badge. 
The syllabuses are designed to be fun and engaging, and they cover a wide range of topics. 
They are self assessed - you can choose to do as much or as little of the syllabus as you like, and you can choose to do it in any order.
The important thing is that you have fun and learn something new!
