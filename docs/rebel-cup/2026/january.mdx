### Athletes

For this challenge I knew I wanted to created a pair of alternative wings for my combat boots!

All that came to mind was pleather and zip-ties... I sketched out a design, and then got to work. I cut out the wings from the pleather, and then used the zip-ties to create boning to give the wings some structure. After an hour on the sewing machine, I had a pair of wings! Next came eyelets... 

I grabbed the hammer and eyelets, and got to work. I had to make sure the eyelets were in the right place, and that they were secure enough to hold the wings in place. After hammering really loudly, I had a pair of wings that were ready to be attached to my combat boots! I took them out for an adventure with my local rebellion!
